# Stand replacement continuation

Issue: #39. Owner: @ResniDev. Consumer/reviewer: @olartgabo.

The renderer exports pure `stand_continuation_blockers(BuildPlan) -> tuple[Blocker, ...]`.
Pass it as `simulate_patch(..., family_validator=stand_continuation_blockers)`.
No domain schema or generated contract changes.

Active support/base/brace roles derive from the three named joint intersections.
The validator checks triangle geometry, lip fold, equal widths, tape topology,
functional-test mass, checkpoints and the bounded operation sequence. Additional
cuts must produce active replacements equivalent to retained original panels.
Historical immutability is checked by the replanner, not inferred from one plan.

Replacement remaps future accessibility references and their cut dependencies.
Original grouped cuts remain preserved when other outputs are useful. For the
compiled stand brace fixture, `lost_part_ids` names the brace, `lost_operation_ids`
is empty, and a `retire_part` delta records its area. Calling the whole three-panel
cut lost would misreport the retained support/base work. Single-output cuts whose
outputs are all retired retain the existing lost-operation semantics.

The manual renders the replacement cut separately and uses active role/feature IDs
in assembly views. Eight core step slots keep stable headings; full visible SVG
content remains the hash input. Golden hashes captured before the change cover
all sixteen panels of two original parameterizations.

Validation results are recorded in the PR. Tests use labelled synthetic stock and
user-report fixtures only; they establish no physical or live-provider pass.

The follow-up resize slice extends original uncut base depth at fixed width,
recomputing brace length and the three-panel layout from recorded margin/kerf.
Confirmed footprint and source bounds remain enforced. Other targets, shrinking,
rotation, declared offcuts, replacement-bearing layouts and completed cuts block.
No original input or material history is mutated. Angle and
joint-strategy regeneration and completed-work undo remain unsupported. Runtime
callback wiring is tracked separately in #40; no plan is committed by this helper.
